ArrowRemove 隐私政策

政策版本：1.0

更新及生效日期：2026年8月21日

运营者：ArrowRemove 在 App Store 产品页面所示的开发者（以下简称“我们”）

联系邮箱：workflow@live.cn

感谢您使用 ArrowRemove（以下简称“本应用”）。本政策说明本应用如何处理与您有关的信息，以及您可以如何管理自己的隐私选择。本政策适用于本应用的 iOS 和 iPadOS 版本。

本应用不要求注册账号。我们不会通过本应用直接要求您提供姓名、手机号、电子邮箱、通讯录、照片、音频、精确位置或支付信息。为保存游戏进度和展示第三方广告，本应用仍会进行下述有限的数据处理。适用法律要求取得同意时，我们会通过应用内的隐私消息或其他适当方式征得您的同意。

简要说明

游戏进度只保存在您的设备上，不会上传至我们的服务器。本应用接入 Google Mobile Ads 和 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）；这些服务可能处理设备、广告、使用情况和诊断数据，以提供广告、衡量效果、维护服务并管理隐私选择。

1. 我们处理的信息

信息类别 具体内容及获取方式 处理目的 保存位置及期限

2. 广告、同意和跟踪说明

  • 本应用在适用地区使用 Google UMP 获取并执行广告相关的隐私选择。只有 UMP 表示可以请求广告时，本应用才启动 Google Mobile Ads 并加载横幅广告。
  • 根据您的地区、选择和广告服务配置，您看到的广告可能是个性化广告或非个性化/情境广告。
  • 本版本没有实现 Apple“App 跟踪透明度”（ATT）授权流程，因此本版本不会基于 ATT 授权访问用于跨 App 跟踪的 IDFA。Google 仍可能处理应用或开发者范围内的其他设备标识符，以及广告和互动数据。
  • 如果未来版本拟开展 Apple 所定义的跨 App 或网站跟踪，我们会先更新本政策，并在跟踪发生前通过 ATT 请求您的明确授权。

3. 第三方服务和信息共享

为提供广告与隐私选择功能，本应用集成以下 Google 服务：

  • Google Mobile Ads：展示横幅广告并进行广告投放、衡量、安全和诊断。
  • Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）：展示适用的隐私消息并管理您的广告隐私选择。

我们不会为金钱对价出售您的个人信息。向 Google 提供广告请求所需信息，在某些司法辖区可能被法律定义为“共享”或用于定向广告的数据处理；我们会根据适用法律和您的选择予以限制。Google 作为独立第三方，会依照其自身政策处理收到的信息。请参阅 Google 隐私权政策 和 Google Mobile Ads 数据披露说明。

除上述情形外，我们仅会在以下情况下披露必要信息：取得您的明确授权；为履行法定义务或响应有权机关依法提出的要求；为保护用户、公众、我们或他人的合法权利和安全；或发生合并、收购、重组等交易且承接方继续受本政策及适用法律约束。我们要求接收用户数据的合作方提供不低于本政策及适用法律要求的保护。

4. 数据跨境处理

Google 在全球运营，其处理的信息可能被传输至您所在国家或地区以外的服务器。Google 负责依据适用法律采取相应的跨境传输和安全保障措施。我们将在适用法律要求时另行告知并取得必要授权。

5. 保存、删除和您的权利

  • 本地进度：您可以通过删除本应用来删除本地游戏进度。本应用目前不设账号或云端存档，因此我们没有可按账号检索的远程游戏进度。
  • 广告隐私选择：在适用地区，如首页显示“隐私选项/Privacy options”，您可以随时打开该入口修改或撤回相关选择。撤回同意不影响撤回前基于同意进行处理的合法性。
  • 访问、更正、删除、限制或反对：您可以通过本政策所列邮箱提出适用法律赋予您的请求。为保护安全，我们可能需要核验请求。由于部分广告数据由 Google 控制，我们可能会指引您使用 Google 的隐私工具，或在法律要求的范围内协助转交请求。
  • 申诉：如果您认为我们的处理不符合本政策或适用法律，可以先联系我们；您也有权向当地有管辖权的数据保护机关投诉。

6. 数据安全

我们采取与数据性质和风险相适应的合理技术与管理措施，并尽量减少数据处理。本应用的游戏进度保存在 iOS/iPadOS 应用沙盒内。通过互联网传输或电子存储无法保证绝对安全；发生依法需要通知的安全事件时，我们会按照适用法律采取补救和通知措施。

7. 未成年人

本应用不以儿童为主要受众，也不面向 Apple Kids Category。未达到所在地法定数字同意年龄的用户，应在父母或监护人同意和指导下使用本应用；在中国境内，不满十四周岁的未成年人应由监护人阅读并同意本政策。如果您是监护人，并认为未成年人在未经适当同意的情况下向我们提供了个人信息，请通过下方邮箱联系我们，我们会依法处理。

8. 政策更新

当应用功能、第三方 SDK、数据实践或法律要求发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策。重大变更会通过应用内提示、更新后的政策页面或适用法律要求的其他方式告知，并在需要时重新征得同意。页面顶部的日期表示本政策最近一次更新和生效时间。

9. 联系我们

如对本政策或个人信息处理有疑问、意见或请求，请发送邮件至 workflow@live.cn。请在主题中注明“ArrowRemove 隐私请求”。我们会在适用法律规定的期限内回复。

ArrowRemove Privacy Policy

Policy version: 1.0

Last updated and effective: August 21, 2026

Operator: The developer identified on ArrowRemove's App Store product page ("we," "us," or "our")

Contact: workflow@live.cn

Thank you for using ArrowRemove (the "App"). This Policy explains how the App handles information relating to you and how you can manage your privacy choices. It applies to the App's iOS and iPadOS versions.

The App does not require an account. We do not directly ask you through the App for your name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos, audio, precise location, or payment information. The App nevertheless performs the limited processing described below to save game progress and display third-party advertising. Where applicable law requires consent, we request it through an in-app privacy message or another appropriate mechanism.

In brief

Game progress remains on your device and is not uploaded to our servers. The App integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), which may process device, advertising, usage, and diagnostic data to provide advertising, measure performance, maintain their services, and manage privacy choices.

Advertising and usage data The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process an IP address (which may be used to infer coarse location), device identifiers (such as an advertising identifier or app-/developer-scoped identifiers), ad impressions and interactions, product interaction, performance data, crash data, and other diagnostics. The third-party SDKs do not necessarily process every listed data type during every use. Actual processing depends on your device, region, SDK configuration, ad requests, and privacy choices.

2. Advertising, consent, and tracking

  • Where applicable, the App uses Google UMP to obtain and honor advertising privacy choices. The App starts Google Mobile Ads and loads banner ads only when UMP indicates that ads may be requested.
  • Depending on your region, choices, and the advertising service configuration, you may receive personalized or non-personalized/contextual ads.
  • This version does not implement Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorization flow and therefore does not obtain ATT-based access to the IDFA for cross-app tracking. Google may still process other app- or developer-scoped device identifiers, advertising data, and interaction data.
  • If a future version proposes tracking across apps or websites as Apple defines that term, we will update this Policy and request your express permission through ATT before tracking begins.

3. Third-party services and sharing

The App integrates the following Google services to provide advertising and privacy choices:

  • Google Mobile Ads: displays banner ads and supports ad delivery, measurement, security, and diagnostics.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): displays applicable privacy messages and manages your advertising privacy choices.

We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. Providing information needed for an ad request to Google may be legally defined as "sharing" or targeted-advertising processing in some jurisdictions; we restrict such processing as required by applicable law and your choices. Google acts as an independent third party and processes information under its own policies. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Otherwise, we disclose necessary information only with your express authorization; to meet legal obligations or respond to lawful requests from competent authorities; to protect the lawful rights and safety of users, the public, us, or others; or in a merger, acquisition, or reorganization where the recipient remains bound by this Policy and applicable law. We require partners receiving user data to provide protection equal to or greater than that described here and required by applicable law.

4. International processing

Google operates globally, and information it processes may be transferred to servers outside your country or region. Google is responsible for applying the transfer and security safeguards required by applicable law. We will provide any additional notice and obtain any authorization required by applicable law.

5. Retention, deletion, and your rights

  • Local progress: You can delete local game progress by deleting the App. The App currently has no account or cloud save, so we do not hold remotely searchable game progress tied to an account.
  • Advertising privacy choices: Where applicable, if "Privacy options" appears on the home screen, you can use it at any time to change or withdraw relevant choices. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.
  • Access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection: You may email us to exercise rights available under applicable law. We may need to verify a request for security. Because Google controls some advertising data, we may direct you to Google's privacy tools or help forward a request where legally required.
  • Complaints: Please contact us first if you believe our processing does not comply with this Policy or applicable law. You may also complain to the competent data protection authority in your jurisdiction.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature and risk of the data and seek to minimize processing. Game progress is stored in the iOS/iPadOS app sandbox. No internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If a security incident requires notice by law, we will take remedial and notification steps as required.

7. Children

The App is not directed primarily to children and is not intended for Apple's Kids Category. Users below the digital age of consent where they live should use the App only with the consent and guidance of a parent or guardian. In mainland China, a parent or guardian must review and agree to this Policy for a user under 14. If you are a guardian and believe a child provided personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us and we will respond as required by law.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the App's features, third-party SDKs, data practices, or legal requirements change. We will communicate material changes through an in-app notice, an updated policy page, or another method required by law, and obtain renewed consent where necessary. The date at the top shows when this Policy was most recently updated and became effective.

9. Contact us

For questions, comments, or requests about this Policy or our processing of personal information, email workflow@live.cn with “ArrowRemove Privacy Request” in the subject line. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.
